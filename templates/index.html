    🤖 Multi-Agent Research Assistant

    This app uses a team of 4 AI agents powered by CrewAI to automatically research any topic, analyse findings, write a structured report, and review it for accuracy — all in under 2 minutes.

    How it works: You enter a research topic, and the 4 agents work in sequence — the Researcher finds information using Tavily web search, the Analyst extracts key insights, the Writer creates a structured report, and the Reviewer polishes the final output.

    Built with:

    🤖 CrewAI
    🔗 LangChain
    ⚡ Groq LLM
    🔍 Tavily Search
    🚀 FastAPI
    🐳 Docker
